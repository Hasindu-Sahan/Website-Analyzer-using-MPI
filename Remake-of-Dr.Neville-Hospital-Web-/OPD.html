Outpatient Department (OPD)

The OPD is situated in the ground floor of the hospital and has a floor area of 64sq meters. It is fully air conditioned and has three separate doctors’ rooms, a dressing room and an injection room. The OPD has a spacious waiting area which can accommodate around twenty patients, and a larger number of patients can be accommodated in the adjoining main lobby area. The three doctor rooms are spacious and each has a Doctor’s table and patient examination bed and water sink with all other standard instruments. The OPD functions 24 hours a day with medical and nursing staff serving on a shift based roster.
